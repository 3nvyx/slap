What is Slap?

Slap is a small device that sits on the professor’s desk. It listens to the lecture and displays live captions on each student’s phone in their preferred language.

One device. The whole room.

Plug it into a laptop or a power bank with USB-C. Slap joins the Wi-Fi and its screen shows the class code and a QR code for the room.

The orange button starts and stops a session. So does a double slap on the case. The recording light is on whenever the microphone is live, so everyone can see it.

Scan the sticker on the device, tap it with NFC, or type the class code. The captions open in the browser on a laptop or phone. No account and nothing to install.

The lecturer's words appear as they are spoken, with each new word in bold. The translation in your language follows a moment later, sentence by sentence.

When the session stops, Slap builds an outline, concepts, flashcards and practice questions from the lecture. Sign in to keep them with your class.
